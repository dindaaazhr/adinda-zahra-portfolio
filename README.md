## Featured Projects

### 1. Early Detection of Customer Spend Decline in TokoIndo

**SQL · Python · Tableau · Predictive Analytics**

Analyzed **2.60 million retail transactions** to identify customers at risk of a **≥30% spending decline over the next eight weeks**. The ExtraTrees model achieved a **test ROC-AUC of 0.711**.

Delivered a three-page Tableau dashboard highlighting spending trends, revenue at risk, and household priorities. The top 20% retention priority list covered **48.4% of historical severe loss**. Proposed a randomized retention pilot to assess spend recovery and net margin.

### 2. Branch–Department Clinical Service Demand and Workforce Review

**Tableau · LOD Expressions · Healthcare Analytics**

Built an interactive dashboard to compare hospital service demand, doctor workloads, monthly admissions, and estimated bed utilization across branches and departments.

Recommended doctor rotation and backup coverage for departments with heavier workloads, using patient-days per doctor to guide staffing decisions.

### 3. Customer Segmentation Based on Sales Performance

**Python · K-Means · RFM Analysis**

Segmented credit card customers using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary features to support campaign targeting and retention. High-Value Active customers represented **27.6% of customers** but generated **nearly half of estimated net transaction profit**.

Developed segment-specific recommendations for reactivation rewards, card usage incentives, and premium merchant benefits.

### 4. RevoGrocers Category Sales Analysis

**SQL · Google BigQuery · Retail Analytics**

Used joins, CTEs, and window functions to analyze category revenue and repeat purchasing. Identified **Confections** as the leading revenue contributor at **12.87%**, with a **99.85% repeat purchase rate**.

Recommended product bundles and targeted loyalty programs based on category performance.

### 5. TokoBli Campaign Strategy Comparison & Product Page A/B Test

**Google Sheets · Statistics · A/B Testing**

Compared promotional campaigns and evaluated a product page experiment. The **12/12 campaign** achieved a **58.54× revenue-to-discount ratio**, while the A/B test showed a statistically significant difference with a medium effect size.

Recommended targeted offers and product bundles, alongside a phased product page rollout to **25%, 50%, and 100%** of users with performance checks at each stage.

### 6. Heejab — Trading Management System

**Java · Java Swing · Microsoft SQL Server**

Developed a desktop trading management application with an interactive graphical interface and persistent database storage.

Combined interface prototyping, event handling, error handling, and SQL Server integration to support trading data management.
